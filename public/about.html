About Mentec

Mentec Foundation was founded with a single belief: students learn best when their tools are simple, fast, and respectful of their time. We build software that gets out of the way so learning can take center stage.

Our mission

To make high-quality learning infrastructure accessible to every institution — from small training centers to large universities.

Our values

  • Clarity — fewer features, done well.
  • Privacy — student data is private by default.
  • Reliability — the portal is always there when you need it.
